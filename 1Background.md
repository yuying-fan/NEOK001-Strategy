## Company and Current Pipeline
NEOK Bio, Inc., is an US biotechnology company focused on antibody drug conjugates (ADCs) development in cancer treatment. The company's prinicipal investor is ABL Bio, Inc., a Korean biotech company. 

NEOK Bio emerged from stealth mode with $75 million in Series A funding. This fund will be used to advance two bispecific ADC programs into clinical trials. The two lead assets are NEOK001 (ABL206) and NEOK002 (ABL209).
- NEOK001 is a ROR1 and B7-H3 bispecific ADC
- NEOK002 is an EGFR and MUC1 bispecific ADC
NEOK has not specified which tumors it'll go after but Mayank Gandhi, Neok’s CEO, mentioned this dual-targeting strategy should be able to target a wide range of indications with large unmet needs. The initial focus will be on thoracic, gastrointesticanl and gynecological cancers, which are known to be sensitive to Topo-1 inhibition.

NEOK Bio will run clinical development and commercialization for the two ADCs, while ABL Bio is the originator and hold the foundational patents.

Currently, as of February 2026:
- NEOK001 received FDA IND approval in January 16, 2026. The plan is to start Phase 1 clinical study by 1H2026 and share initial clinical data in 2027. NEOK001 is NEOK's first program to enter clinical development.
- NEOK002's IND application was submitted January 30, 2026 to the FDA and is pending approval.
