# Valuation Assumption

rNPV requires many inputs you probably don’t have publicly (true manufacturing cost, final indication pricing, exact timelines, probabilities of success, partnering terms). That’s fine — do a transparent assumptions table and show low / base / high scenarios and one-way sensitivity (e.g., peak sales, probability of success, discount rate).

Deliverable you can produce now: A scenario rNPV with 3 scenarios (conservative / base / optimistic), a tornado chart of sensitivities, and a short appendix listing the assumptions and their sources/justifications.

rNPV = Present value of expected future net cashflows, where each future cashflow is multiplied by the probability that the program reaches that cashflow (i.e., probability of clinical/regulatory success from current stage), and then discounted to today.
